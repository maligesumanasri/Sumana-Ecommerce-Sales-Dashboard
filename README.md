# Project Objective

The goal of this project is to analyze the overall **E-commerce sales performance** and gain insights into profits, quantities sold, customer behavior, and category-wise contribution.
This dashboard helps businesses understand key sales trends, profitable categories, and top-performing regions to make data-driven decisions for future growth.

# Questions (KPIs)

1. What is the **total amount, profit, quantity, and average order value (AOV)**?
2. Which **state** contributed the most to the total sales?
3. Which **product category** generated the highest revenue?
4. What is the **monthly trend of profit** throughout the year?
5. Which **customers** purchased the most products?
6. Which **payment mode** was most frequently used by customers?
7. Which **sub-category** gave the highest profit?
8. How do sales vary across **quarters and states**?

---

# **Process**

* Verified and cleaned the dataset to remove missing or inconsistent values.
* Ensured data consistency in terms of format, datatype, and naming conventions.
* Used **Power Query** for data transformation and filtering.
* Created multiple visuals including bar charts, donut charts, and column charts.
* Combined all visuals into one **interactive dashboard** with slicers for **quarter and state** filters.
* Added KPIs at the top for a quick overview of total Amount, Profit, Quantity, and AOV.

## **Project Insights**

* **Total Sales:** ₹438K | **Total Profit:** ₹37K | **Total Quantity:** 5615 | **AOV:** ₹121K
* **Top States:** Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, and Uttar Pradesh contributed the highest sales.
* **Category-wise Performance:** Electronics category dominates the revenue share.
* **Payment Trends:** COD and Credit Card are the most preferred payment modes.
* **Monthly Trends:** Highest profit observed during the months of **January** and **October**.
* **Top Sub-categories:** Printers and Bookcases are the most profitable items.

---

## **Final Conclusion**

To boost overall profitability and sales:

* Focus marketing campaigns on **top-performing states** like Maharashtra and Uttar Pradesh.
* Continue to **promote Electronics** and high-profit subcategories such as Printers and Bookcases.
* Encourage customers to use **digital payment methods** through offers or discounts.
* Monitor monthly sales trends to identify **seasonal patterns** and plan inventory accordingly.
